## 2026-07-23 - Increase Checkbox Hit Area
**Learning:** Wrapping checkboxes and their associated text in a `<label>` tag significantly increases the clickable hit area, making the interface more accessible, especially on touch devices or for users with motor impairments.
**Action:** Always ensure form inputs like checkboxes and radio buttons are nested within or explicitly linked (via `for` and `id`) to a `<label>` element.
