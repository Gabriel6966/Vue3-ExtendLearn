## 2026-06-18 - Improve Clickable Hit Area
**Learning:** Wrapping checkbox inputs and their corresponding text inside a <label> tag significantly improves accessibility and usability by increasing the clickable hit area, a crucial detail often missed when using span elements for text.
**Action:** Ensure all custom checkbox or radio implementations group the input and text within a <label> tag or use explicit 'for' and 'id' attributes.
