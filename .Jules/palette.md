## 2025-02-27 - Increase Hit Area for Form Elements
**Learning:** Wrapping native form inputs (like checkboxes) and their descriptive text inside a <label> tag significantly increases the clickable hit area, making the interface much more accessible and easier to use, especially for users with motor impairments or on mobile devices.
**Action:** Always wrap checkboxes and radio buttons with their corresponding text in a <label> element rather than placing them adjacently without a shared container.
